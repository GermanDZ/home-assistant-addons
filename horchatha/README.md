# HorchatHA

![Supports aarch64 Architecture][aarch64-shield] ![Supports amd64 Architecture][amd64-shield]

*Horchata is what Valencia pours for guests. HorchatHA welcomes yours.*

A self-hosted bridge that brings a **cloud video intercom** into Home Assistant:
ring events per door, live video, visitor photos, door opening and the
door-open history, all as native entities over MQTT discovery.

It signs in to your own intercom account as one more client of it. Not
affiliated with or endorsed by any intercom manufacturer or provider.

See [DOCS.md](DOCS.md) for setup.
